What It Does

Anyone can download the forms. The hard part is made easy by FamilySoft.

Keeping names, dates, and numbers consistent across a whole set of connected documents, and staying current as Washington keeps changing them. FamilySoft handles both.

  • Worry-free format standards. Confidence your forms meet court format standards — so you avoid rejections.
  • Only what's relevant. Automatically clear away messy checkboxes with the “condense” feature — it strips excess unused verbiage and consolidates fragmented phrases into clean sentences, leaving only what matters for this client and this matter.
  • Always current. Washington updates its forms and calculations regularly. FamilySoft keeps them current, so your client's information is always expressed in the latest forms, never an outdated version.
  • Accurate calculations. Child support and financial numbers calculated using Washington's approved formulas.
  • Consistency across forms. Enter the case facts once — or have your client enter them — and that information carries across related pleadings, declarations, and orders.
